About the hackathon

CodeRun brings students together to turn practical ideas into small, working software projects.

Our mission

We give student developers a supportive place to learn by building, share ideas, and get feedback from mentors.

Who is it for?

Frontend and backend developers, mobile developers, and designers are welcome. You do not need prior hackathon experience.

Team requirements

  • One to three members per team
  • A working prototype to present
  • Use the tools and frameworks that fit your idea

Sponsors and mentors

Local technology companies support the event with mentors, workshops, and opportunities for standout teams.
